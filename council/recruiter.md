# Council seat: senior in-house recruiter (200 searches)

## 1. Three things that would make me open this daily

1. **Paste the JD, get a ranked shortlist with a reason.** Not the animation — the *why now* line. "Came 2nd to a stronger backend hire at PayNord in May; wanted more ownership; comp within band" is the sentence I paste to my hiring manager. If that line is accurate, I use this tool. It replaces 40 minutes of scrolling my own ATS rejects.
2. **The Story tab — where they finished second and the recorded reason.** That is the data that dies in every ATS I have used; debriefs live in someone's head or a scorecard nobody reads. If this is the place that reason survives, it earns its login.
3. **Warmth decay + follow-up reminders.** I don't forget people because I'm lazy; there's just no surface saying "you liked her, it's been 90 days." Amber at 14 days and sequences that become reminders is the actual product. Keep "never sends silently" — I will not let a tool email my market.

## 2. Three things wrong or missing by week two

1. **No "why not now" states — and that's most of my bench.** I need filterable statuses the radar and scoring respect: *went silent* (3 touches, no reply → stop ranking her #1), *took another role* (snooze to a date; auto-resurface at ~18 months tenure with "worth a ping"), *do not re-approach* + reason (values fail, HM said never, she asked us to stop), *opted out / erasure request*. Today someone who took another job pulses amber forever and I stop trusting the amber.
2. **The deciding data isn't in the schema.** Notice period; visa/sponsorship need; actual comp *at the time of that process* with its date (a 2024 number is a lie in 2026); location and on-site days; current employer + tenure start (my best timing signal); the req and hiring manager they lost to; consent/source date for GDPR. Plus seniority drift — a strong Senior in 2024 is a Staff candidate now, and the score should say so.
3. **Local-first IndexedDB is a week-two disaster, and 12 candidates is not a bench.** I use a laptop and a desk machine; my bench is 800–3,000 people and two colleagues touch it. Single-browser storage means no dedupe/merge on import, no "Ravi already got a message from Tom on Tuesday", no backup when IT reimages me, no bulk actions (multi-select 30 → snooze, tag, export). The force-layout radar is also unreadable past ~80 nodes: ship the ranked list as the landing view, radar as a toggle.

## 3. Cut

**The ROI strip.** "Agency fees avoided" is a number I'd have to defend to my VP and can't; "hours saved" is vendor theatre. Give me one honest counter — *placements sourced from the bench* — and nothing else.

## 4. What I'd say to a colleague after a month

"It's the only place the reason someone didn't get the job actually survives — just don't trust the pretty bubble screen, live in the list."
